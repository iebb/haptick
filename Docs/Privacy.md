# HapTick Privacy Policy

HapTick does not collect personal data. It has no account system, advertising, analytics, or tracking service.

Timer settings and haptic compositions are stored locally on your device. When you pair an Apple Watch, the app exchanges these settings and playback commands with that Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework.

If you enable the wrist-flip control on Apple Watch, motion data is processed on the Watch to detect the gesture. The app does not send motion data to a server.

Deleting the app removes its local settings. A paired device may keep its own settings until you delete the app on that device as well.

For support or privacy questions, open an issue at [iebb/haptick](https://github.com/iebb/haptick/issues).
