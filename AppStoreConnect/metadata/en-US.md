# HapTick App Store Metadata - en-US

Name: HapTick

Subtitle: Haptic timer for Apple Watch

Promotional Text:
Set a repeating haptic pulse on Apple Watch, then tune the interval or BPM from either your watch or iPhone.

Description:
HapTick is a focused haptic timer and silent metronome for Apple Watch.

Set a repeat interval or BPM, choose the Watch haptic style, and start or stop the pulse directly from the watch face. The iPhone companion app gives you a larger control surface for editing the same settings and sending them to Apple Watch.

Features:
- Repeating Apple Watch haptics at a chosen interval or BPM
- Interval mode and BPM mode with clear limits
- Multiple Watch haptic styles
- Optional flip gesture to start or stop
- iPhone companion controls synced to Apple Watch
- Saved settings, so your previous timer is ready next time

HapTick is designed for simple, private timing cues when you want a tactile metronome rhythm without sound.
