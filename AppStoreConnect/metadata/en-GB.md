Name: HapTick

Subtitle:
Haptic loops on iPhone & Watch

Promotional Text:
Compose a repeating haptic sequence on iPhone, play it locally, or sync it to Apple Watch for an independent silent metronome.

Description:
HapTick is a haptic timer and silent metronome for iPhone and Apple Watch.

Set an interval or BPM, choose a single haptic style, or compose a sequence of up to 64 steps. Each step uses its haptic style abbreviation. Change, move, duplicate, or remove steps, then loop the whole sequence.

Play haptics while HapTick is open on iPhone, or sync your settings and composition to Apple Watch. The Watch saves its settings and works independently. Turn the Digital Crown to adjust the interval or BPM, tap the ring to start or stop, or enable the optional wrist-flip gesture.

Speed warnings help you choose a suitable interval for every style in your sequence.

Available in English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French, and Spanish. No account, advertising, analytics, or sound required.
